# Arches for JU Excavations

This is the MN Lab deployment of Arches for Science (AfS), adapted to document and manage archaeological excavation data.

## What MN Lab adds

The MN Lab implementation extends the base AfS deployment with workflows for:

- defining and assigning excavation coordinate systems;
- importing GNSS and total-station measurements, and publishing basemap overlays;
- creating and documenting 3D and IIIF digital resources;
- annotating 3D models and IIIF images;
- importing and presenting RTI resources;
- recording pottery collections and individual pottery records; and
- a customised landing page, reports, viewers, and map components for excavation work.

Use the **Workflows** section in the navigation to follow each process step by step.

## Technical basis and attribution

The deployment code is maintained in the [AFS for MN Lab Excavation repository](https://github.com/mn-lab-platform/afs-for-mn-lab). It is a fork of [OpenContext's `arches-via-docker`](https://github.com/opencontext/arches-via-docker), adapted by MN Lab for excavation workflows.

The repository retains the upstream AGPL-3.0 license and source history. This site documents MN Lab-specific functionality and user workflows.
