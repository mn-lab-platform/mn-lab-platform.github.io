# About Arches for JU Excavations



**Arches for JU Excavations** is designed to be the digital foundation of your project, ready to be set up the moment you break ground at a new site.



Built upon the powerful, open-source [Arches Project](https://www.archesproject.org/) framework, it acts as a comprehensive, all-in-one environment tailored specifically for the realities of archaeological fieldwork. The system comes with its own pre-configured, specialized data models, integrated mapping, and intuitive upload workflows ensuring that complex field data entry is as seamless and effortless as possible from day one.



## Core Pillars of the Application



### Ontology-Driven Data Models

To guarantee semantic richness and global standardization, we developed a custom archaeological data model strictly adhering to the **CIDOC CRM (v7.1.3)** ontology, including the CRMarchaeo, CRMgeo, and CRMdig extensions. The system is populated with tailored resource models reflecting real-world archeological entities (such as Trenches, Contexts, and Special Finds), allowing researchers to accurately map physical excavations to digital archives without losing semantic meaning.



### Intuitive Data Entry Workflows

Archaeological data entry shouldn't require a computer science degree. We replaced standard database forms with a custom set of guided, step-by-step workflows to streamline the upload process. Whether you are importing Total Station data, assigning a local coordinate system, or uploading a 3D model, these simplified forms ensure that complex data is captured consistently and effortlessly directly in the field.



### Integrated Spatial Mapping

Advanced geospatial analysis is built directly into Arches for JU Excavations. Our custom **Map Plugin** allows archaeologists to visualize and customize the appearance of resources overlaid on top of self-uploaded, high-resolution basemaps (such as aerial drone imagery). You can interact with and visualize spatial data in a continuous 2D MapLibre-based workspace without needing to switch to a separate GIS application.
