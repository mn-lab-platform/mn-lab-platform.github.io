## Add Digital Resource 3D

This workflow allows you to upload and link a 3D model to an existing resource in your database. It is designed to take a 3D model package (specifically 3D Tiles format) and automatically create a new Digital Resource, linking it directly to a parent resource of your choosing.

---

### Step 1: Select Resource

The first step requires you to choose the parent resource that your new 3D model will be linked to. 

*   **Search for a Resource:** Use the **"Search resources..."** input bar at the top to filter available resources by name.
*   **Select a Resource:** The available resources are displayed as clickable cards in a grid layout. Click on the card of the resource you wish to use. 
*   **Confirm Selection:** Once clicked, the card will be highlighted in blue with a checkmark in the top right corner. A green **"Selected:"** confirmation box will also appear at the top of the screen displaying the name of your chosen resource.

---

### Step 2: Add 3D Model

Once your parent resource is selected, you will move on to uploading the actual 3D model file and defining its basic details.

*   **Upload the File:** In the designated dropzone panel, you can either drag and drop your file or click the **"Choose ZIP or 3TZ file…"** button. 
    *   *Note:* The system strictly accepts 3D Tiles packaged in either **.zip** or **.3tz** formats.
*   **Name the Model:** In the settings section below the upload area, type the desired name for your new 3D resource into the **"Model name:"** text field.
*   **Submit:** Once the file is queued and the name is entered, click the blue **"Upload 3D Model"** button at the bottom of the section to process the upload.

> **[IMAGE PLACEHOLDER: Step 2 UI]**
> *Highlight suggestion: Highlight the dashed-border dropzone area, the "Model name:" text input field, and the blue "Upload 3D Model" submit button.*

---

### Step 3: Summary

After a successful upload, you will be brought to the final Summary screen.

*   **Confirmation:** You will see a green success checkmark and a **"3D model added successfully!"** message. 
*   **View your Resource:** You can immediately inspect your newly created Digital Resource by clicking the blue **"View Resource"** button. This will open the new resource's dedicated page in a new browser tab.
